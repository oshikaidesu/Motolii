# Motolii visual language

**Swiss housing. Toybox contents. Things have faces. Identity travels; bodies change.**

This is a language for deriving new UI, not a parts list. It contains no sizes, colours or radii. Those live in `motolii/ui/lib/theme/metrics.dart` (the one visual-metrics canon) and may change without this language changing.

The housing is quiet and disciplined. What it holds is not. Create holds toys for shapes, Relations hold toys for relationships, Colour holds colour itself, Effects hold toys for phenomena. Colour is not reserved for one category.

## 1. Housing

Order inherited from Swiss and Ableton: grid, alignment, type, density, a dark casing. The housing does not make claims of its own. It never competes with what it holds, and it should almost vanish when you squint.

## 2. Things have faces

A thing is not reduced to a generic control. Text, Cube, Scatter, Colour and Blur have faces that reveal what they are or what they do, before a label does. Generic operations (save, search, a toggle, a menu, a plain number field) may remain generic. If a thing has no face yet, finding its face is the design work.

## 3. Toybox

Tools should make you want to touch them. Colour, shape, glyph and phenomenon may be bold. A professional tool does not have to be monochrome.

## 4. Identity travels

The same thing is recognisable wherever it appears. Recognition comes from what it is, not from copying how it looks. Scatter should be unmistakably Scatter in every place, without any rule that fixes its colour area, shape or size per place.

## 5. Place changes the body

Each place asks the thing for the body that is most natural there.

- Browser: a body for discovering.
- Inspector: a body for operating.
- Stage: a body that appears in space.
- Timeline: a body that appears as time.

Do not unify components. Unify identity.

## 6. Phenomenon before parameters

Show the effect before the number. Scatter shows scattering before 0.72. Colour shows colour before RGBA. A path shows a line before coordinates. Numbers stay available as precision, second.

## 7. Fold, do not miniaturise

Do not pack complexity smaller. Only the thing being worked on opens into an instrument. Everything else folds and keeps its identity visible.

## 8. The work stays loudest

This does not mean a plain UI. The work is photographic and high-frequency. The UI is graphic and relational. That difference separates them without lowering anyone's colour.

## Deriving a new thing

Use this when a feature arrives that the concept never drew (for example Physics). Answer in order and stop when the answers are clear.

1. What is it? One sentence without UI words.
2. What phenomenon is its face? What does it look like when it acts?
3. In the Browser: what toy is it? What makes someone want to pick it up?
4. In the Inspector: what instrument shows the phenomenon first? Which few numbers sit second?
5. On the Stage: how does it appear in space, if it does?
6. In the Timeline: how does it keep its identity as time?
7. Folded: what small living face remains?
8. Check against sections 1 to 8. Does the housing stay quiet? Does the work stay loudest?

If a step needs a size, colour or radius to be answered, that step is not yet understood.

## What this language deliberately does not say

It does not fix any colour, size, radius, shadow rule, or which category may use colour. Those are properties of a particular build and are recorded as observations.
